# Solar-powered-sterlization
Solar Powered Sterilizer
1st Place — Medha Medical Device Hackathon 2K24
Prototype Category | KIET Group of Institutions | Mentored by BETIC, IIT Bombay

Overview
A solar-powered medical sterilization device designed to provide clean, safe surgical and medical tools in rural areas and disaster zones where grid power is unavailable or unreliable.
The system uses solar energy to drive both thermal and UV-based sterilization, achieving 85–90% sterilization efficiency with zero dependency on the electrical grid.

Problem Statement
In rural India and disaster-affected areas, hospitals and health workers often lack reliable electricity. Unsterilized medical tools are a leading cause of post-surgical infections. This project addresses that gap by building a portable, self-powered sterilization unit that works entirely on solar energy.

Circuit Design
The system consists of the following key components:

Solar Panel — Converts sunlight into DC electrical energy
Charge Controller — Regulates voltage and prevents battery overcharge
Battery — Stores energy for operation during low-light conditions
Heating Element — Raises chamber temperature for thermal sterilization
UV Lamp — Emits UV-C radiation (200–280 nm) to kill pathogens
Control Circuit — Timer and temperature-based control to manage sterilization cycles
Sterilization Chamber — Enclosed compartment for medical tools


Key Results

Achieved 85–90% sterilization efficiency using combined thermal + UV method
